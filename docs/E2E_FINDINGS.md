## Added 22 Oct 2026: the first real "Create order" (MET-0026) made a draft in the wrong company, with no tax rows
The owner pressed Create order on the Stainless Steel Sheet request (7,000 Kg, changed from 5,839 first): the approval, proposal and work order were recorded correctly and ERPNext got Draft **PUR-ORD-2026-00736**. 1. **Wrong company.** `config/settings.erpnext.yaml` had no `erp.company`, and this ERPNext has two companies (ABC Corp (Demo) and Test Trading Co), so ERPNext used the service account's default company: the draft was for ABC Corp, with `Stores - ACD` and ABC's cost centre, while the tax template and the item belong to Test Trading Co (it could not have been submitted cleanly).

## Added 24 Oct 2026: cancel or change an order from Telegram, chat and the console
